# RideNite demo: pitch notes

- Every reload starts a fresh demo. Nothing is saved on the phone between loads, so each walkthrough begins from the same sample state (Maison Cheryl tab linked, saved home set, no ride booked).
- iOS Safari may reload a backgrounded page on its own, for example after switching apps for a while. That reload resets a mid-trip ride back to the start. This is intentional for the demo, not a bug.
- If that happens mid-pitch, tap Ride home again; the ride books in two taps.
- To reset on purpose: Menu, then Reset demo data, or long press the SIMULATED DEMO label. Reset is the same as a reload.
- All venues, guests, drivers, plates, prices and messages are fictional sample data. No real rides, payments, texts or POS connections happen.
